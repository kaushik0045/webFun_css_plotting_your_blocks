Get Ready

What To Bring To Your Appointment

Having these on hand makes your filing faster and more accurate.

For Individuals
  • Photo ID
  • Social Security card or ITIN
  • Prior-year tax return (if available)
  • W-2s and 1099s
  • Deductible expense records (mortgage interest, medical, charitable donations)
  • Health coverage forms (1095-A/B/C), if applicable
For Businesses
  • Business formation documents
  • Profit & loss statement
  • Business expense records
  • Prior-year business return (if available)
  • Payroll records, if you have employees
