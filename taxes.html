Your Credential

What Is an Enrolled Agent?

An Enrolled Agent (EA) is the IRS's highest credential for tax professionals — federally licensed by the U.S. Department of the Treasury, not limited to a single state the way a CPA license is.

Licensed by the IRS

Enrolled to practice before the IRS by the U.S. Department of the Treasury — not licensed state by state like a CPA.

Earned Through Rigorous Testing

EA status requires passing a comprehensive three-part IRS exam covering individual returns, business returns, and representation.

Unlimited Representation Rights

Can represent taxpayers before the IRS in audits, collections, and appeals — a right shared only with attorneys and CPAs.

Nationwide, Ongoing Education

Must complete continuing education to keep the license active, and can practice before the IRS in any state.
