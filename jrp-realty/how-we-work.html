How We Work

Our 7-step process

A disciplined, repeatable approach that protects investor capital from first look to final sale.

01

Step 1: SourceFind the right opportunity

We search for income-producing properties in markets with steady demand: apartments, hotels, and commercial land.

02

Step 2: UnderwriteRun the numbers, then run them again

Rents, expenses, financing, and the local market are stress-tested. If it doesn’t work on paper, we walk away.

03

Step 3: Due DiligenceInspect everything

Physical inspections, financial audits, title, and legal review, so there are no surprises after closing.

04

Step 4: Raise & FinanceBring partners together

Investors join as Limited Partners and we secure financing on terms that protect cash flow.

05

Step 5: AcquireClose the deal

As General Partner, JRP closes on the property and starts the business plan on day one.

06

Step 6: Manage & ImproveAdd value every month

We oversee operations, upgrades, and leasing, and send investors regular updates and distributions.

07

Step 7: ExitReturn capital and profits

When the plan is complete, we refinance or sell and share the profits with our investors.
