How We Work

Our seven-step process

A disciplined, repeatable approach that protects investor capital at every stage, from first look to final sale.

  1. 1Step 1: Source

    Find the right opportunity

    We search for income-producing properties in markets with steady demand, including apartments, hospitality, and commercial assets.

  2. 2Step 2: Underwrite

    Run the numbers, then run them again

    Every deal is stress-tested: rents, expenses, financing, and the local market. If it doesn’t work on paper, we walk away.

  3. 3Step 3: Due Diligence

    Inspect everything

    Physical inspections, financial audits, title, and legal review, so there are no surprises after closing.

  4. 4Step 4: Raise & Finance

    Bring partners together

    Investors join as Limited Partners. We secure financing on terms that protect the property’s cash flow.

  5. 5Step 5: Acquire

    Close the deal

    JRP, as General Partner, closes on the property and puts the business plan into motion on day one.

  6. 6Step 6: Manage & Improve

    Add value every month

    We oversee operations, upgrades, and leasing to grow income, and send investors regular updates and distributions.

  7. 7Step 7: Exit

    Return capital and profits

    When the plan is complete, we refinance or sell the property and share the profits with our investors.
