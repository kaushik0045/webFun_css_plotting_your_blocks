About Us

About JRP

A California real estate investment firm built on one idea: investors deserve the rewards of real estate without the headaches.

Our Story

Rooted in California. Focused on investors.

JRP Realty (JRP Investment Group) gives investors access to the kind of real estate that used to be reserved for large institutions: apartment communities, hotels, and commercial projects across California.

Many people love the stability of real estate but don’t want to be landlords. Through syndication, investors pool their capital and we act as General Partner, sourcing, acquiring, managing, and eventually selling each property.

From our office in Modesto, we focus on assets with durable demand and dependable cash flow.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver steady, passive income and long-term growth to our investors through carefully selected real estate, with all the work and none of the stress on their side.

We get there through careful underwriting, disciplined planning, and hands-on management of every property we own.

Our Vision

“The Central Valley’s most trusted real estate investment partner.”

We aim to be the first call for investors, sellers, and communities in California’s Central Valley by delivering value, integrity, and results on every project.

Our Values

TRANSPARENCY  |  INTEGRITY  |  STABILITY  |  PARTNERSHIP  |  DISCIPLINE

We treat every investor dollar as our own, report openly, and only pursue deals we would invest in ourselves.
