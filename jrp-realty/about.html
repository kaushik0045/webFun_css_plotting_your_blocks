About Us

About JRP Realty

A California real estate investment firm built on one simple idea: investors should get the rewards of real estate without the headaches.

Our Story

Rooted in California. Focused on investors.

JRP Realty (also known as JRP Investment Group) was founded to give everyday investors access to the kind of real estate that used to be reserved for large institutions.

Many people like the stability of real estate but don’t want to be landlords. We bridge that gap with a syndication model: investors pool their capital, and we act as General Partner, sourcing, acquiring, managing, and eventually selling each property.

From our office in Modesto, we invest in apartments, hospitality, and commercial properties chosen for durable demand and dependable cash flow.

Our Mission

Steady, passive income for our investors, without the stress of owning property.

We put investor capital into carefully chosen income-producing real estate, then manage every detail so our partners can focus on their lives, not their properties.

Our Vision

To be the Central Valley’s most trusted partner for real estate investors.

We want every investor to know exactly where their money is, how it is performing, and why each decision was made.

Our Values

TransparencyIntegrityStabilityPartnershipDiscipline

We treat every dollar as if it were our own, report openly, and only pursue deals we would invest in ourselves.
