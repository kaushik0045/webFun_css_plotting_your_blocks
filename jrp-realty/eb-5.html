EB-5 Investment Program

Invest in the U.S. Build your future here.

The EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program offers a path to a U.S. green card through a qualifying investment.

What is EB-5?

A path to permanent residence

The EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program lets qualifying investors obtain U.S. permanent residence (a green card) by meeting the investment requirements set by U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).

Your questions answered

Who qualifies for the EB-5 program?

The EB-5 program is designed for investors outside the United States who want to live, invest, and build wealth in the U.S.

How does the EB-5 investment work?

USCIS reviews both the investment and the investor. The investment must be at least $800,000 in a Targeted Employment Area (TEA) project or $1,050,000 in a non-TEA project. It must come from lawful sources, stay invested through the I-829 petition, and create 10 permanent full-time U.S. jobs.

Investors must also have a clean record, free of criminal convictions, immigration violations, and fraud.

Can my family come with me?

Yes. Approved investors, their spouses, and unmarried children under 21 can relocate to the United States, in some cases in as little as two years.

What if my application is denied?

Refiling and appeals are often possible. Consult your immigration attorney about the best course of action.
