Real Estate Syndications

A collective way to build wealth

In a syndication, investors combine their funds to buy and operate commercial real estate. It’s a hands-off way to enjoy the perks of property ownership without the work of managing it.

Our core expertise is multifamily real estate. Every opportunity we offer is evaluated for growth potential, profitability, and sustainability, and our team guides both first-time and experienced investors.

Common questions

What is a real estate syndication?

A partnership where investors pool money to buy and operate a commercial property together. The General Partner (JRP) manages everything; Limited Partners (investors) share in income and profits without day-to-day work.

Do I need to manage anything?

No. As a Limited Partner your role is passive. We handle acquisition, management, and reporting.

How do I earn money?

Typically through periodic distributions from the property’s cash flow, plus a share of profits when it is refinanced or sold. Returns are not guaranteed.

What are the tax benefits?

Owners of real estate can often deduct depreciation, which may offset some taxable income from distributions. Ask your tax advisor about your situation.

Who can invest?

Eligibility depends on each offering and on securities rules. Some offerings are limited to accredited investors. Contact us to discuss current opportunities.

How long is my money invested?

Hold periods vary by property, often several years. Each offering’s documents describe the expected timeline.
