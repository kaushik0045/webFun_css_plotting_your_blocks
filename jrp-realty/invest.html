FAQ

Common questions

What is a real estate syndication?

A partnership where several investors pool money to buy a property together. The sponsor (General Partner) runs the investment; investors (Limited Partners) share in income and profits.

Do I need to manage anything?

No. As a Limited Partner your role is passive. JRP Realty handles acquisition, management, and reporting.

How do I earn money?

Typically through periodic distributions from the property’s cash flow, plus a share of profits when it is refinanced or sold. Returns are not guaranteed.

What are the tax benefits?

Real estate owners can often deduct depreciation, which may offset some of the taxable income from distributions. Ask your tax advisor about your situation.

Who can invest?

Eligibility depends on each offering and securities rules. Some offerings are limited to accredited investors. Contact us to discuss current opportunities.

How long is my money invested?

Hold periods vary by property, often several years. Each offering’s documents describe the expected timeline.
