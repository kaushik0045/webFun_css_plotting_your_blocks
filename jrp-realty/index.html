Welcome to JRP Realty

Your partner for smart, hands-off real estate investing.

Based in Modesto, California, JRP Realty works with investors who are tired of low returns from savings accounts and bonds, or uneasy about stock market swings.

We pool investor capital to buy quality income-producing properties: apartment communities, hospitality, and commercial assets. As the General Partner, we handle everything: finding the deal, financing, closing, management, and the eventual sale.

As a Limited Partner, you get the benefits of owning real estate (steady income, growth, and tax advantages) without tenants, toilets, or late-night calls.

Our Mission

Steady, passive income for our investors, without the stress of owning property.

We put investor capital into carefully chosen income-producing real estate, then manage every detail so our partners can focus on their lives, not their properties.

Our Vision

To be the Central Valley’s most trusted partner for real estate investors.

We want every investor to know exactly where their money is, how it is performing, and why each decision was made.

Our Values
