Welcome to JRP Realty

Your partner for hands-off real estate investing.

Based in Modesto, California, JRP Investment Group works with investors tired of low returns from savings and bonds, or uneasy about stock market swings.

We use a syndication model: investors pool their capital and we handle everything, from sourcing and acquiring to managing and improving multifamily, hospitality, and commercial properties.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver steady, passive income and long-term growth to our investors through carefully selected real estate, with all the work and none of the stress on their side.

We get there through careful underwriting, disciplined planning, and hands-on management of every property we own.

Our Vision

“The Central Valley’s most trusted real estate investment partner.”

We aim to be the first call for investors, sellers, and communities in California’s Central Valley by delivering value, integrity, and results on every project.

Our Values

TRANSPARENCY  |  INTEGRITY  |  STABILITY  |  PARTNERSHIP  |  DISCIPLINE

We treat every investor dollar as our own, report openly, and only pursue deals we would invest in ourselves.

Who We Are

JRP Investment Group is a California real estate investment firm. As the General Partner (Sponsor) we carry the detail and the responsibility of every deal, so our Limited Partner investors enjoy a truly hands-off income stream.

About Us

What We Do

We acquire and manage income-producing real estate: apartment communities, hotels, and commercial projects. Returns are anchored in tenant leases and real operating businesses, governed by strict state and federal laws.
